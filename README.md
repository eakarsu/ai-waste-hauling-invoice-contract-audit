# Waste Hauling Invoice & Contract Audit

Audit waste and recycling invoices against containers, service events, weights, disposal rates, and contract fee terms.

**Primary buyer:** Multi-site businesses and property portfolios. **Evidence:** hauling agreements, containers, service schedules, lifts, weights, disposal tickets, contamination, fuel and environmental fees, invoices, and credits.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Hauling contract library
- Location container registry
- Scheduled service calendar
- Service event ingestion
- Missed pickup credit
- Haul charge calculation
- Weight ticket validation
- Disposal rate audit
- Contamination evidence
- Fuel surcharge validation
- Environmental fee control
- Invoice recalculation
- Hauler dispute workflow
- Credit reconciliation
- Location waste analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4718>. API: `5718`.
